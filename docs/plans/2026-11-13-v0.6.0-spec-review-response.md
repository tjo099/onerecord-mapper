# v0.6.0 spec, review round 1: findings and dispositions

Spec r1 (`e53a486`) was reviewed by three independent reviewers:

- **AR**: architecture and compatibility (`comprehensive-review:architect-review`)
- **ON**: IATA ontology fidelity (general-purpose, verified against the 3.2.0, 3.3.0 and 3.2.1 TTLs and the IATA docs)
- **IM**: skeptical implementer (general-purpose, with bun/tsc/Zod experiments)

All three confirmed every cited line number, range, cardinality and code
value. Dispositions below: **A** accepted, **P** partially accepted (reason
given), **R** rejected (reason given). Spec r2 carries every change.

## Scope changes (the biggest outcome)

r1 deferred `bookingTimes` and `bookingSegments` and ignored inherited
properties. The reviewers showed both cuts were wrong or poorly justified
(AR1, ON2, ON7, IM2).

The r1 reason for deferring ("would need a second shape beside the legacy
class") does not hold, because the embedded shapes are internal. r2 makes
the six chain classes **complete per 3.2.0**: own restrictions plus those of
intermediate superclasses. The only exception is the four
`:LogisticsObject`-level properties, a package-wide gap now documented as
deviation #19. Added:

- **`TransportLegs`** LogisticsObject class and codec (AR1). P7 builds FLT/RTG from it.
- **Embedded `BookingTimes`** on BookingOption and Booking.
- **Embedded `BookingSegment` and `PieceGroup`** on Booking. AR recommended keeping these in Path A; r2 includes them anyway, because the same "internal shape" argument applies and space allocation (`spaceAllocationCode`) is central to booking confirmation.
- **Inherited `:LogisticsService` properties on Booking**: `activitySequences` (embedded `ActivitySequence`), `contactDetails` (embedded `ContactDetail`), `contactPersons` (IRI). The `contactPersons` precedent is `Company`, `Composing` and `UnitComposition`.
- **Enums** `SpaceAllocationCode` and `TransportMeansServiceType`.

## Round 2: verification of r2 (one reviewer, general-purpose, with experiments)

There were no blockers. The reviewer confirmed:

- every new r2 ontology claim, checked against the official 3.2 file: the
  ten `TransportLegs` properties, the five added embedded shapes, the three
  `LogisticsService` properties, the `LogisticsObject`-level list, and the
  `SpaceAllocationCode` and `TransportMeansServiceType` values;
- 3.2 and 3.3.0 are identical for every in-scope class;
- the `XSD_DURATION` regex is correct, tested on 26 inputs;
- the npm-alias oracle works under `bun --frozen-lockfile` with a single zod
  copy;
- declaration emit keeps `@deprecated` on hand-written types;
- coverage has headroom.

All round-1 dispositions landed. Spec r3 applies the following:
